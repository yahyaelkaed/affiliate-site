    ## 1. EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max — Best Overall 2kWh Power Station

    The **EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max** provides a 2,048Wh base capacity, expandable up to 6,144Wh using extra batteries. Its 2,400W continuous AC output (4,800W surge) can run essential home appliances during power outages.

    ### Key Specifications
    - **Capacity:** 2,048 Wh (LiFePO4)
    - **Continuous Output:** 2,400W (Surge 4,800W)
    - **Cycle Life:** 3,000 cycles to 80%+ capacity
    - **Solar Input:** 1,000W Dual MPPT
    - **Weight:** 50.7 lbs (23 kg)

    ### Discharge & Runtime Performance
    In testing, the DELTA 2 Max powered a 150W full-size residential refrigerator for 13.5 hours continuously. It features quiet fan operation under 30dB at low loads.
